## Task
Standard-mode twin of `roboarc-rope-threading-02-i01-privileged` (same frozen instance): the agent gets only the `eai-standard/2.4` client
(docs/STANDARD_MODE.md); the simulator runs in the `sim` sidecar (`environment/docker-compose.yaml`, GPU on the
sidecar), which owns the scene and records the episode as a (T, 14) trajectory; its collect hook
(`environment/sim/finalize.sh`) replays the trajectory once in a fresh process for success, rendering the video during
that replay. The verifier runs in a separate sandbox (`tests/Dockerfile`) over the sidecar's artifacts. Compare against
the privileged task to measure the cost of losing resets, privileged state and the tooling's primitives.

## Difficulty
Same scene, instance and success rule as `roboarc-rope-threading-02-i01-privileged` (see its Difficulty). In standard mode the agent loses resets, privileged state and planning primitives: it sees camera images and proprioception only, acts through the client, and gets one episode, so it has to estimate the scene from images and recover from its own mistakes without starting over.

## Details

### Goal
Thread the rope through the cyan ring (R1) then green ring (R2) then purple ring (R3), in that order along the rope from material start to material end.

### Success requirements
- Required directions along that material order: cyan ring (R1): cross toward the side containing the green ring (R2); green ring (R2): cross away from the side containing the cyan ring (R1); purple ring (R3): cross away from the side containing the green ring (R2). Do not reverse the crossing directions.
- Pass fully through each required hole exactly once, with no extra crossings or ambiguous tangencies. Keep the full rope thickness clear of the rim, with an additional 0.1 mm radial margin; the passage must clear both sides of each ring's thickness, not just its middle plane.
- Keep every rope point at or below 10 mm/s. Holding is allowed; release is not required.
- Keep the rope intact; collapsed or inverted rope volume is not accepted.
- Keep all final conditions satisfied together for at least 0.5 s of simulation time. If any final condition stops holding, the confirmation interval starts again. A brief qualifying pose is not enough; let the simulation advance through the full interval.

### Grader details (in addition to the requirements above)
- The rope is a chain of 200 rigid capsule links (5.5 mm long, 8 mm thick) joined end to end; each joint bends and twists against a spring set by the rope's material, with damping, and stops bending at 0.9 rad, where neighbouring links touch. The links do not deform: nothing about the rope's shape or volume ends the episode. A link moving faster than 6 m/s or spinning faster than 300 rad/s is slowed by extra damping (a speed clamp); this is not reported and does not end the episode. Pulled harder than about 997 N, the rope's links get extra rotational inertia (a tension guard), which only removes energy and is not reported.
- Robot safety (this benchmark's tooling): no gripper target goes lower than 15 mm below the tabletop; a commanded target that would bring any part of a gripper lower is stopped there, on the commanded path. The fingertips reach about 16 mm beyond the tool frame (TCP) along its approach axis, open or closed. Arm drive torques are capped (the effort limits in robot_spec), so an arm pushing against an obstacle stalls at that torque. An arm with any part more than 5 mm deep in a fixed object (a fixture, the table) is stopped where it is: its joint targets become its measured joints for that control step, so it stops pushing (the response does not report these stops).
- Forcing is recorded, not judged: an arm drive (not a gripper drive) at 95% of its torque cap or more while that arm's gripper, or a part it holds, presses on anything else with 20 N or more in total is forcing; 0.5 s of it on every physics tick is a forcing event in the evidence (`meta["safety"]`), shown to the reviewer when a success is reviewed. Forcing does not end the episode and does not change the grade by itself.
- Simulator breakdown (a MuJoCo instability: a non-finite or diverging acceleration, position or velocity): the episode ends as an infrastructure error, not your failure, unless an arm was forcing (even for one tick) within the 0.25 s before it: then it is your failure (forced_breakdown: what broke and what forced it).
- Sizes (rope radius 4 mm): R1: inner radius 69 mm, tube radius 6 mm, centreline passage radius 65.9 mm on the middle plane and 68.2 mm on the two tube-offset planes; R2: inner radius 69 mm, tube radius 6 mm, centreline passage radius 65.9 mm on the middle plane and 68.2 mm on the two tube-offset planes; R3: inner radius 69 mm, tube radius 6 mm, centreline passage radius 65.9 mm on the middle plane and 68.2 mm on the two tube-offset planes.
- Aperture check: round_ring, compression allowance 1 mm. This allowance changes the geometric passage test; the collision geometry stays as declared.
- The material start is the rope end that is farther from the rings at the start.
- Only the grippers' fingers collide with the rope and the rings: every other robot part (palms, wrists, arm links, bases) passes through them without contact, so only the fingers can push, pinch or hold the rope.
- Grasp regions (assisted grasping, see Grippers above; one or more spheres per graspable part): rope: 200 spheres of radius 8 mm. Both finger contacts must lie inside one of the part's spheres; a part touched elsewhere is grasped physically.
- Grading: success needs the live episode to succeed (with the thresholds above) AND one fresh-process replay of its rows to succeed; a live failure is final whatever the replay shows. This family's physics runs on the CPU (MuJoCo, float64), so that replay reproduces the live episode on the same host; the rule is kept as a margin: it runs the rows with the thresholds above and, if the episode has not ended after the last row, holds the final targets for up to 2 s (50 control steps), stopping at the first success or failure, so a confirmation that starts a few physics ticks later than in the live episode can still complete; the hold uses the thresholds above too (no tolerance for this family). Nothing else changes.
