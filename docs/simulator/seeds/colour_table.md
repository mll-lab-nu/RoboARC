## How the colours were obtained

- **Verified from code, by generating the assets.** For each base and seed I ran the task class's `declare()` (current TopoGym dev code, `.venv-gs121-nyx`, one thread) into a scratch folder with that seed's task parameters. I then read the colours it wrote: URDF `<material><color>`, MJCF visual-geom `rgba`, GLB `baseColorFactor`, and each EntitySpec's `surface.color`. This covers interlocked-claws-01..13, cast-keyhole-01..06, cast-coil-01..08, buzz-wire-3d-01..10, fiber-bundling-01..10, hedgehog-in-cage-07, orbits-01/02, rope-threading-01..08, slotted-post-ring-01..05, battery-replacement-01 and caliper-sorting-01. Nyx draws from these files (`nyx_direct.py` builds its scene from the task's EntitySpecs and generated assets); the frozen `scene.xml` holds only colliding geoms.
- **Refresh at `a849170272`.** Between `f1659a3904` and `a849170272`, `topogym/` changed only in six files: `battery_replacement.py`, `caliper_sorting.py`, `scene_visuals.py` (two new rope surfaces), the claws `common.py`, `interlocked_claws_cast_trinity.py` and `scene_variation.py`. I re-ran the `declare()` dumps on the new HEAD for battery-replacement-01, caliper-sorting-01, interlocked-claws-13, interlocked-claws-06 (a check of the claws `common.py` change; its colours are unchanged) and rope-threading-01..08. In those runs every seed's parameters were recomputed with `scene_variation.appearance(cls, S)` from the new code. The dumps of the other families still apply.
- **Seed parameters** (new code):
  - battery: s1 = controller white, tray grey, box blue, props wood; s2 = controller "red" (the Pulse Red controller lying face down: white back, red bumpers and triggers), tray white, box green, props black. "blue" (Shock Blue) is not used by seeds 1 or 2.
  - caliper: s1 = fixture forest_green, bins dark_red, sleeves brass; s2 = safety_orange, dark_green, black_oxide.
  - interlocked-claws-13: other_rgb blue / green and third_rgb green / blue for s1 / s2.
  - rope-threading: s1 blue_rope, s2 black_rope.
  - Every other base: taken from `release_instances/<base>/seeds/<S>/instance.json` (`derived_from.appearance`); these match `release_1.2.0/work/REPORT.md` and the new code.
  - Stale derived instances: those of rope-threading-01..08 (orange/golden rope), caliper-sorting-01 (pale bins) and interlocked-claws-13 (no third_rgb) carry pre-refresh parameters. battery-replacement-01's parameter names are unchanged, but the colours they map to changed. All four need re-deriving before the seeds ship.
- **Green dashed goal region.** The colour comes from a code constant, `topogym/assets/geometry/goal_region.py` (surface colour (0.10, 0.85, 0.20)). No appearance axis touches it. `PlacementGoalMixin.finalize_spec` adds the region, so it was not part of the `declare()` dumps. The frozen `scene.json` of all 39 bases whose texts mention a green dashed region/square has a `goal_region` entity. Knot-untying-07/09 do not draw the region in the scene. Their texts point to `/app/goal/region_cam_*.png`, and the `region_images.json` of seeds 1 and 2 (`<host work dir>/seed_derive_work/goal/knot-untying-0{7,9}-i01/seeds/{1,2}/`) records the marker colour [0.1, 0.85, 0.2].
- **Rendered frames, looked at only as a cross-check.** I rendered nothing new.
